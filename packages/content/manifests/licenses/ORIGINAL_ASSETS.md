# Original runtime art and audio

Revision: original-procedural-1. Created 2026-10-22 by the Fender Offenders project. Reviewed: 2026-10-24 for `arcade-v8`.

*Locally grown geometry, freshly synthesized boings, no mystery box of borrowed hubcaps.*

Every runtime car, building, tree, road marking, sign and material is generated by the project's TypeScript source. There are no downloaded visual archives, external textures, branded vehicles or third-party fonts. Source paths are `packages/content/src/index.ts` and `packages/renderer/src/`. The 2026-10-26 [patrol sound bank](audio/README.md) adds CC0 engine/foley recordings and original arrangements; this original-only record applies to project-authored assets and the historical synthesis baseline, not those third-party recordings.

These original assets are licensed under **GNU GPL v3 or later (`GPL-3.0-or-later`)**, together with the original repository code and documentation; see [LICENSE](../../../../LICENSE) and [the project notice](../../../../README.md#license). They are not third-party CC0 assets. Generated geometry has no external download/archive hash to invent. Preserve project copyright/license notices when distributing covered work. Dependencies retain their own licenses; compatibility/notices and release packaging remain a separate Q07 audit.

The five cases’ fifty mugshots are original SVG paths, palettes and procedural accessory recipes in `packages/contracts/src/campaign-mugshots.ts`. The original ten portraits are preserved; forty further portraits use varied faces, hair, clothing and original chef/guide/watch/sailor details. Names and the five storylines are authored in `packages/contracts/src/campaign.ts` and `extra-storylines.ts`; callsign word lists live in `callsigns.ts`. Portraits contain no external images, scripts, fonts or runtime requests. All follow the same GPL-3.0-or-later terms.

Grenade bodies, fuse smoke, blast warning patterns, explosion geometry and throw/fuse/blast sounds are original procedural source in `packages/renderer/src/grenade-effects.ts` and `audio.ts`. No new third-party media or recordings were added with armed campaign targets.

Noon/dusk/night palettes, fog and directional lighting in `packages/renderer/src/environment.ts`, plus the generated diffuse street-light texture/material integration in `street-light-field.ts`, are original authored source too. Shallow-water sites in `packages/content/src/water.ts`, PBR water/borders in `wet-surfaces.ts`, procedural source halos/headlights in `light-detail.ts`, contact masks in `contact-shading.ts` and the car/city surface responses are also original project source under GPL-3.0-or-later. They introduce no downloaded sky textures, HDR maps or flare images. Optional planar reflections/AO remain disabled in [the graphics polish sequence](../../../../spec/GRAPHICS_POLISH.md).

Cars use Y-up, meter units, front +Z and centered pivots in shared coordinates; renderer adapters convert shared X exactly once. Building collision footprints use the same authored positions and dimensions as visible walls. Roof/facade details do not enlarge those footprints. Sidewalks/markings are presentation. Trees, lamps, signs, benches, bins, barrels and crates have [separate authority-owned rigid scenery](../../../../spec/WORLD_REACTIONS.md); their one-way car interaction adds no vehicle damage or handling impulse.

Pedestrian figures, routes, flying wreck pieces and the historical synthesized cues are original source work too. Current sampled wreck sounds are documented separately in [audio provenance](audio/README.md). Cosmetic figures have no health or combat role. Source attribution and AI-assistance acknowledgments are in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](../../../../CONTRIBUTORS.md). The record establishes provenance and chosen terms; it does not certify the full dependency or release-package audit.

The enlarged avenue layout, twelve additional buildings and outer street lamps in `packages/content/src/index.ts` / `packages/renderer/src/city.ts`, plus oil footprints, warning rings and hubcap blades in `packages/renderer/src/road-gadgets.ts`, are original project-authored geometry under the same GPL-3.0-or-later terms. No artwork, models, audio or other media were imported from Spy Hunter or another game.
