# Cosmetic borough pedestrians

`PedestrianCrowd(scene)` owns24 original low-poly, instanced figures. Call `update(renderedVehicles, dt, reducedMotion)` after sampling the rendered car poses, `reset()` when leaving/restarting a world, and `dispose()` with the renderer. `getStats()` reports total, visible, walking, running, waiting, hiddenSafety and cumulative emergencyRetreats. The class converts shared X coordinates to Babylon X exactly once; do not parent it under the city's reflected coordinate root. No colliders, health, hit reactions, targeting or authoritative state are created.

The independent authored route module is `packages/content/src/pedestrian-routes.ts`. Eight loops follow clear sidewalks and the actual zebra crossings at road-centre offsets10.2m. Crossing starts wait for predicted car gaps. Approaching traffic causes early running on navigable pavement; someone already crossing retreats along that zebra to a safe endpoint. Every rendered vehicle contributes a previous/current swept path and turning/reversing forecast. A final conservative4.7m presentation guard includes cartoon vehicle stretch and swinging arms. Late snapshots or teleports can require an immediate move to the nearest validated pavement refuge. If every refuge is occupied, the cosmetic figure is temporarily suppressed rather than rendered struck. Normal lane traffic should use running/waiting without such emergency relocation; it is counted for QA. Reduced motion removes figure bob/lean/limb swing while preserving all avoidance.

Focused tests: `pnpm exec vitest run packages/renderer/src/pedestrian-motion.test.ts packages/renderer/src/pedestrians.test.ts`.

Native capture: `node packages/renderer/dev/capture-pedestrians.mjs`. It starts a private Vite server5186 and closes its own browser afterward. The isolated fixture imports city and car art but never the renderer entry point. It saves `work/pedestrians-walk.png`, `work/pedestrians-flee.png` and a JSON report. Acceptance: recognizable contrasting shirts/heads, articulated walking/running, figures grounded on pavements/crossings, readable separation from cars, no page errors, all24 visible and no safety hiding for the ordinary demonstration. No physical or multiplayer pedestrian simulation is implied; each client's cosmetic routes may differ with its rendered car stream.
