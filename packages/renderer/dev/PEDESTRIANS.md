# Cosmetic borough pedestrians

Reviewed: 2026-10-26 for `arcade-v10`. *Twenty-four residents, all firmly opposed to becoming a gameplay mechanic.*

`PedestrianCrowd(scene)` owns 24 original low-poly, instanced figures. Call `update(renderedVehicles, dt, reducedMotion, suspectId?)` after sampling rendered car poses, passing `frame.pursuit?.suspectId`; call `reset()` when leaving/restarting a world, and `dispose()` with the renderer. `getStats()` reports total, visible, walking, running, waiting, hiddenSafety and cumulative emergencyRetreats. The class converts shared X to Babylon X exactly once; do not parent it under the city's reflected coordinate root. No colliders, health, hit reactions, targeting or authoritative state are created.

The independent authored route module is `packages/content/src/pedestrian-routes.ts`. Eight loops follow clear sidewalks and zebra crossings at road-centre offsets of 10.2 m. Crossing starts wait for predicted car gaps. Only active player vehicles and the identified suspect cause early running on navigable pavement; someone already crossing retreats along that zebra to a safe endpoint. Identity, rather than body style, determines this threat. Every rendered vehicle still contributes a previous/current swept path and turning/reversing forecast for crossing waits and overlap safety. A final conservative 4.7 m presentation guard includes cartoon vehicle stretch and swinging arms. Late snapshots or teleports can require an immediate move to the nearest validated pavement refuge; ordinary traffic does not start the running animation even during this fallback. If every refuge is occupied, the figure is temporarily hidden. Normal lane traffic should use walking/waiting without emergency relocation; QA counts each retreat. Reduced motion removes bob/lean/limb swing while preserving avoidance.

Focused tests: `pnpm exec vitest run packages/renderer/src/pedestrian-motion.test.ts packages/renderer/src/pedestrians.test.ts`.

Native capture: `node packages/renderer/dev/capture-pedestrians.mjs`. It starts a private Vite server on 5186 and closes its browser afterward; Playwright Chromium or installed Chrome is required. The isolated fixture imports city and car art but never the renderer entry point. It saves `work/pedestrians-{walk,traffic,flee,suspect}.png` and a JSON report. Acceptance: ordinary traffic causes no running, officers and suspects do cause running, recognizable contrasting shirts/heads, articulated walking/running, grounded figures, readable car separation, no page errors, all 24 visible and no safety hiding in the ordinary demonstration. Each client's cosmetic routes may differ with its rendered car stream.

Campaign weapons and rigid props do not make people damageable. Continue checking safe separation against every sampled player/traffic/target pose, including discontinuities. Human visual approval and sustained crowd/frame costs remain in [the acceptance queue](../../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md#next-acceptance-work); the fixture is no substitute for a full busy-district route.
